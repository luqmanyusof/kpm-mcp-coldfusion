## **Introduction: Why Build an MCP for Your Web App?** 

Most web apps are built for people. You log in, click around, fill forms, press buttons. The app is a set of doors, and a human must walk through each one. Nothing happens unless someone is there doing it. 

An MCP (Model Context Protocol) changes who can use those doors. It lets an AI assistant operate your app like a trained employee would — instantly, tirelessly, in plain English. Think of it as a “staff entrance” the AI uses, alongside the front door for humans. 

The picture is simple. Instead of clicking fifty times to add fifty products, you tell the AI: “Add these products from the supplier’s email and flag any already in stock.” It does the work for you. Your app still holds the data and enforces the rules — people just talk to it instead of operating it. 

MCP is the “USB-C moment” for AI and software. Before, connecting an AI to an app meant fragile, custom work every time. Now you build it once, and any AI that speaks MCP can plug straight in. 

**The key benefits and advantages:** 

- **Talk instead of click** — get things done by asking, not navigating menus. 

- **Automate routine work** — data entry, updates, cleanups, and lookups are handed off. 

- **Build once, connect anything** — a standard any MCP-capable AI can use. 

- **Your app stays in control** — the same rules, validation, and permissions apply. 

- **Empower non-technical staff** — work gets done without knowing every button. 

- **Faster, fewer errors** — repetitive tasks run quickly and consistently. 

- **Safe automation** — the AI works through your guardrails, not around them. 

- **Future-ready** — as AI improves, your app already works with it. 

_That’s exactly what this course builds — end to end._
